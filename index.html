A little about us

From the fields of Pakistan, with purpose.

HAZAR Impex Ltd is a Lahore-based rice export company connecting the character of Pakistani rice with the needs of international buyers.

We work with established milling partners in Gujranwala and Larkana to bring authentic Basmati and non-Basmati varieties to market. Every order starts with a conversation about your specifications, destination and the quality your customers expect.
